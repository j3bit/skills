## SaaS / Software

Look for:

- ARR
- RPO or cRPO
- billings
- NRR or expansion behavior
- large-customer adds
- seat growth
- usage expansion
- AI upsell or module layering
- gross margin
- operating leverage
- FCF leverage

## Semis / Infrastructure / Hardware Platforms

Look for:

- design wins
- content per unit
- hyperscaler or enterprise capex
- backlog
- lead times
- attach rates
- networking or system pull-through
- supply allocation changes
- gross margin and mix
- evidence that demand is not just a short-term shortage distortion

## Marketplaces / Internet Platforms

Look for:

- GMV
- take rate
- ad monetization
- payments or fintech cross-sell
- logistics density
- cohort behavior
- order frequency
- basket improvement
- ecosystem reinforcement across multiple business lines

## Pharma / Life Sciences

Look for:

- script growth
- capacity expansion
- supply normalization
- reimbursement progress
- label or indication expansion
- adherence or persistence
- channel access
- evidence that growth duration is extending, not just front-loaded

## Industrial / Energy Transition / Equipment

Look for:

- bookings
- backlog
- book-to-bill
- capacity utilization
- installed-base monetization
- service mix
- project pipeline
- margin reinforcement
- whether the acceleration is structural or just cyclical recovery

## Other sectors

If the sector is not listed above:

- identify the sector's equivalent leading indicators
- explain why those indicators are the right analog
- use them before scoring the company

## Positive patterns

Search for evidence that forward expectations are being rewritten upward, not just that current growth is high.

Look for:

- repeated beat-and-raise behavior
- faster estimate revision velocity over time
- expanding growth duration
- new growth engines stacking onto old ones
- monetization of previously under-monetized usage
- distribution unlocks
- ecosystem flywheels
- capacity unlock meeting real demand
- adjacency expansion with improving economics
- management language shifting from cautious to structurally confident
- price confirmation that follows rather than replaces fundamental evidence

## Useful archetypes

Use these as pattern-recognition prompts, not proof:

- multi-engine layering
- monetization flip
- capacity unlock plus real demand
- ecosystem flywheel
- platform-shift infrastructure buildout
- channel unlock
- regulatory or reimbursement unlock
- adjacency expansion with better margins and visibility

Do not overfit to famous historical winners.

## Anti-false-positive checks

Test whether the apparent acceleration is fake, low quality, or temporary.

Check whether the opportunity is really:

- a cyclical rebound
- an inventory refill or shortage distortion
- channel stuffing or demand pull-forward
- pricing-only growth with weak volume, usage, or engagement
- acquisition-driven growth masking weak organic growth
- one-customer or one-contract concentration
- a temporary regulatory or reimbursement quirk
- margin weakness hidden by adjusted metrics
- stock-based compensation or cash burn masking poor economics
- a narrative stock with price strength but no real forward revision support
