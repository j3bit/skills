# Output Templates

Use the template that matches the request. Adapt wording, but keep the structure crisp and evidence-backed.

## Contents

- A. Screen / Hunt
- B. Single-company deep dive
- C. Top-down theme request
- D. Comparison request
- E. Fast answer mode

## A. Screen / Hunt

| Rank | Ticker / Company | Classification | One-line thesis | 2-4 hard data points | Jerk Potential | Recognition / Crowding | Why now | Key catalyst | Top disconfirming risk |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |

Then add:

- **Best hunting zone summary:** explain why the top-ranked names are early rather than obvious
- **Missing data:** identify any names that remain provisional because evidence is thin

## B. Single-company deep dive

Use this structure:

### Thesis

Write one concise paragraph.

### Initial verdict

- explain why it may or may not be a future jerk-growth company

### Evidence by category

- **Business acceleration**
- **Expectation / revision acceleration**
- **Price confirmation / crowding**

### Scorecard

- **Jerk Potential:** X/100
- **Recognition / Crowding:** Y/100

### What must happen in the next 2-4 quarters to confirm

- bullet list

### What would falsify the thesis

- bullet list

### Missing or weakly observed data

- bullet list

### Final classification

- use one of the standard labels from the main skill

## C. Top-down theme request

Use this structure:

### Theme

- concise description of the shift

### Value-chain map

- upstream
- enablers
- bottlenecks
- downstream beneficiaries

### Where expectation revision is most likely underappreciated

- identify 2-4 nodes in the chain
- explain why the market may still be early there

### Shortlisted companies by role

| Role | Company | Why this node matters | Why expectations may still be low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Timing judgment

- explain why the market may still be early
- or explain why the story may already be late

## D. Comparison request

Use this structure:

| Company | Business acceleration | Revision acceleration | Price confirmation | Jerk Potential | Recognition / Crowding | Final label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

Then conclude with:

- **Best candidate**
- **Second-best candidate**
- **Why the others rank lower:** name the specific failure point

## E. Fast answer mode

If the user wants speed:

- still produce a ranked shortlist
- keep it concise
- clearly label assumptions
- mark the result **provisional** if primary-source evidence is thin

Use this compact format:

| Rank | Company | Why it may be early | Jerk Potential | Recognition / Crowding | Biggest caveat |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

Then add:

- **Assumptions**
- **What to verify next**
